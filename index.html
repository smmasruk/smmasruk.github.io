About Me

I am S.M. Masruk Uddin, a Master's graduate in Space Sciences from the Observatoire de Paris–PSL. My research focuses on galactic archaeology, globular clusters, and data-driven astrophysics. I work with Gaia, APOGEE, HST data, Bayesian inference (MCMC), and neural networks. I am currently seeking a PhD position in Astrophysics.
