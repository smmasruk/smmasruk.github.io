What's next

New chapter

PhD in the StarDance group, INAF Arcetri, Florence

StarDance is an ERC-funded project on the non-canonical evolution of stars in clusters. It tests whether exotic populations such as blue stragglers, red stragglers and hot subdwarfs are the by-products of binary and multiple stellar evolution.

This follows directly from my Master's work. I spent a year measuring ages of 50 Galactic globular clusters from colour–magnitude diagrams and isochrones. Exotic stars are defined by where they sit in those same diagrams, relative to what single-star evolution predicts.

Master 2 · Lab Insertion Unit and Internship

Revisiting the Age Scale of Galactic Globular Clusters

GEPI/LIRA · Observatoire de Paris · Supervisors: Misha Haywood, Laia Casamiquela, Daniel Reese
  • Analyzed astrometric, spectroscopic and photometric datasets (Gaia, APOGEE, HST) to derive precise ages for 50 Galactic globular clusters (HUGS catalog).
  • Applied Bayesian inference with SPInS (MCMC) for stellar parameter estimation, and independently validated results with a neural network for fast age estimates.
  • Investigated the age–metallicity relation to separate in-situ from ex-situ clusters.
Jan – Jun 2024
Master 1 · Internship

Gravitational Waves from Eccentric Orbits around Sgr A*

LUTH · Observatoire de Paris · Supervisor: E. Gourgoulhon
  • Modelled GW signals from compact objects in eccentric orbits using the Black Hole Perturbation Toolkit.
  • Compared Schwarzschild and Kerr cases, exploring the effects of spin, eccentricity and semilatus rectum on gravitational waveforms.
  • Assessed LISA detectability and parameter constraints for such sources.
